Build Your Emergency Fund

Before investing a single naira, you need a safety net. An emergency fund covers unexpected costs without debt. Target 3–6 months of essential expenses in a separate, liquid account.

1Calculate monthly essentials (rent + food + transport + utilities)
2Multiply by 3 for your minimum target
3Open a dedicated account — keep it separate from spending
4Automate a transfer the day after salary lands

💡 Best options: Kuda savings, PiggyVest SafeLock, or any account offering ≥10% p.a.

📊 Budgeting When Prices Keep Rising

Inflation makes budgeting harder but far more important. Here's how to stay on top of it.

🔁
Update monthly

Never copy last month's figures. Prices shift — your budget must too.

🛡️
Build a buffer

Add a 5–10% price-shock buffer inside your needs bucket.

💵
Dollar hedge

Consider a small dollar-denominated savings pot (PiggyVest Dollar Flex) to protect against naira depreciation.

📱
Track in real time

Log expenses immediately — don't rely on end-of-month memory to categorise spending.
