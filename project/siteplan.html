WDD 131 — Website Site Plan

01

Site Name

₦ NairaWise

NairaWise

NairaWise combines "Naira" — Nigeria's national currency — with "Wise," signalling smart, informed decision-making. The name is immediately recognisable to a Nigerian audience, communicates financial purpose without jargon, and is short enough to be memorable and brandable.

Optional domain: nairawise.ng or nairawise.com.ng

02

Site Purpose

NairaWise is a three-page informational and interactive website designed for young Nigerian professionals between the ages of 22 and 35 who want to take control of their personal finances.

  • Provide culturally relevant, jargon-free financial education on budgeting, saving, and investing within the Nigerian economic context.
  • Offer an interactive Budget Planner tool where users enter their monthly income and expenses to receive an instant personalised analysis with actionable status feedback.
  • Curate a Resources page featuring savings strategies, investment options available in Nigeria (T-Bills, mutual funds, FGN Bonds), and links to reputable financial tools.
  • Deliver a daily rotating financial tip to encourage consistent engagement and habit formation.
03

Scenarios

The following questions represent the core questions a target audience member would bring to this site:

  1. "I earn ₦200,000 a month. After rent, food, and transport, I have almost nothing left. How can I start saving?"

    Answered by: the Budget Planner tool and the 50/30/20 budgeting framework on the Home page.

  2. "I have ₦100,000 sitting idle in my account. What safe investment options exist for a Nigerian beginner?"

    Answered by: the Resources page — specifically the Investing section covering T-Bills, FGN Bonds, and mutual funds.

  3. "How much of my income should I be saving each month, and how do I know if I'm on track?"

    Answered by: the Budget Planner's savings rate calculator and the Home page savings pillar section.

  4. "What is an emergency fund, and how do I build one on a modest Nigerian salary?"

    Answered by: the Resources page — Emergency Fund section with a step-by-step savings guide.

04

Color Schema

The palette draws on deep forest greens (trust, growth, stability) paired with warm gold accents (wealth, Nigerian cultural warmth) on a natural cream base (readability, elegance).

Forest Dark #0d3b2e

Header background, footer, section accents, primary text headings

Forest Mid #1a6b4a

Navigation hover states, link colours, stat strip background

Leaf Green #2ea878

Primary buttons, active nav links, section labels, savings bar fill, form focus ring

Naira Gold #d4a843

Tip-of-day border, logo accent colour, quote highlights

Cream Base #faf7f0

Page background, text on dark backgrounds, card backgrounds

Ink #1a1a1a

Body paragraph text, form labels

05

Typography

Two Google Fonts are used — one elegant serif for display and one refined sans-serif for body copy. This pairing creates authority without coldness.

Display Font — Headings & Logo

Playfair Display

Smart Money Habits for Young Nigerians

  • All h1–h4 headings
  • Logo wordmark
  • Pull-quote / tip text
  • Stat numbers on homepage strip

Import: Playfair Display — weights 400, 700

Body Font — All Text Content

DM Sans

Practical, culturally grounded financial guidance — from budgeting your salary to building lasting wealth in the Nigerian economy.

  • All body paragraphs and lists
  • Navigation links
  • Form labels and inputs
  • Captions, footnotes, section labels
  • Buttons

Import: DM Sans — weights 300, 400, 500
