The Framework

The 50/30/20 Rule — Nigerian Edition

Popularised globally but fully applicable to Nigerian salaries. Split your take-home pay into three buckets.

🏠

50% — Needs

Rent, food, transport, utilities, healthcare, and school fees. If this exceeds 50%, it is your first priority to reduce — not your savings.

🎉

30% — Wants

Entertainment, dining out, clothing, data subscriptions, and lifestyle choices. Enjoy life — but within this defined boundary.

💰

20% — Savings

Emergency fund, investments, debt repayment, and long-term goals. This is non-negotiable — automate it the moment your salary lands.
