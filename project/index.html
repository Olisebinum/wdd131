The Framework

The 50/30/20 Rule — Nigerian Edition

Popularised globally, fully applicable to Nigerian salaries. Split your take-home pay into three intentional buckets — and watch clarity replace financial anxiety.

Calculate My Split

50%

Needs

Rent, food, transport, utilities, healthcare. If this exceeds 50%, reducing it is your first financial priority — not your savings rate.

30%

Wants

Entertainment, dining out, clothing, subscriptions. Enjoy life fully — within this defined, guilt-free boundary.

20%

Savings & Investment

Emergency fund, T-Bills, mutual funds, debt repayment. Non-negotiable — automate it the moment your salary lands.
